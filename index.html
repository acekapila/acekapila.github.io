How anyone audits an AI system

Organisations are deploying AI faster than anyone has agreed how to assure it. The old control frameworks half-fit. The new ones, NIST AI RMF and ISO 42001, are still being translated into things an auditor can actually test.

Most of what exists is legal commentary or vendor marketing. Very little of it tells you which questions to ask or what evidence to request. That gap is what I'm working through, against NIST CSF 2.0 and APRA CPG 234 as the starting point.
